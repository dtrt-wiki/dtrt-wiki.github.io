Koller

A blind, Czech-accented Fixer operating from a workshop deep in the Backstreets. Offers medical diagnostics, augment installation, and information brokering. Speaks with a distinctive Slavic accent, frequently using the Czech word pritel (“friend”).

Personality

  • Jovial and eccentric. Greets people with casual warmth, uses pritel liberally. Has a dark sense of humour and does not mince words about the state of his clients (“He looks like he is dying”).
  • Surprisingly competent. Despite his blindness, operates computers, games, and surveillance feeds. Maintains a functioning underground workshop with medical and cybernetic equipment.
  • Independent. Not part of the Community but offers his services to them. Maintains his own client network and supply chains.
  • Business-minded. Will not work for free except for diagnostics. Has a clear augment installation policy: “bring the prosthetics, I install them.”

Physical Description

Blind. Wears an eyemask over his eyes and headphones when sleeping. Speaks with a Czech-influenced accent. Typically found in his workshop chair with a bong nearby. Prosthetic hands that he swaps out from plastic bags.

Workshop (“The Time Machine” / Koller’s Bunker)

Located deep underground, accessible via a rickety elevator that descends ominously. The workshop features:

  • Ceiling: Dozens (approaching a hundred) prosthetic and cybernetic arms hanging like ornaments
  • Workstations: Multiple workshop stations and a surgery area
  • Living area: Low couch, two beanbag chairs, flatscreen TV
  • Computer station: Half a dozen monitors, keyboards, tablets, phones connected by DIY wiring
  • Bedroom: A small door behind which Koller was once found eating ice cream on the toilet

Affiliation & Network

  • Oufi Association: Acts as a subcontractor for Oufi contracts. Handles indemnity payments (purple parcels, 100,000 SAU each per recent contract).
  • The Community: Offers services but is not a member. Ben provides directions to his office.
  • Devyat 9 (The Stray Dogs): Has dealt with them in the past. Considers them “bad for business.”
  • Mysterious client with white hair: Exchanges packages — Koller receives items and provides green vials in return. The client appears to be a Fixer of some seniority.
