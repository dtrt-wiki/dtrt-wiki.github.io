Death’s Head

A biker gang syndicate that controls territory in the Backstreets of District 12 — the campaign’s primary location. They operate through intimidation, extortion, and force, controlling checkpoints, patrols, and warehouse districts. Their reach expanded significantly after the L Corp. collapse (the “fall of the Wing”).

Hierarchy

The Death’s Head operates with a military-like structure:

  • Leader — Unknown to the players. The overall leader has not been revealed.
  • Road Captains — Sector commanders who control the four quadrants of Death’s Head territory. Four known Road Captains:
    • Jodi (Southwest) — Killed by the Kurokumo Clan for failure to keep her territory in line. The Kurokumo Clan set an example following the death of Stray Dogs Packleader Bosco.
    • Velder (Northeast) — Dressed like a welder. Killed by Agent Es in his campaign against the Death’s Head. Part of a different campaign.
    • Unnamed (Northwest) — No details known.
    • Unnamed (Southeast) — No details known.
  • Sergeant-at-arms — Enforcers and lieutenants. Brick served as Sergeant-at-arms under Jodi.
  • Armored Bikers — Elite members, heavily armored, serving as the backbone of offensive and defensive operations.
  • Prospects — Low-ranking members and foot soldiers.

Territory

The Death’s Head controls the Backstreets of District 12, divided into four sectors:

  • Southwest — Controlled by Road Captain Jodi (deceased)
  • Northeast — Controlled by Road Captain Velder (deceased)
  • Northwest — Controlled by unnamed Road Captain
  • Southeast — Controlled by unnamed Road Captain

Key locations include:

  • Checkpoints — Toll gates at territory borders, manned by armed guards
  • The Pound — Prison/detention center (see below)
  • Warehouse district — Storage and supply areas
  • Patrols — Armed groups patrolling streets

Their territory is in direct conflict with the Community, a civilian settlement also in District 12.

The Pound

The Pound served a dual purpose:

  • Death’s Head detention center — A prison location to send troublemakers and criminals
  • Stray Dogs recruiting center — The Stray Dogs would look for new talents to possibly take into their ranks

This mixture created unease, and the Death’s Head reluctantly kept the Pound running under the Stray Dogs’ orders. The Stray Dogs used the Pound as a pipeline for recruiting, while the Death’s Head maintained it as a prison — an arrangement that bred resentment.

Members

Prospects (Foot Soldiers)

  • Wear leathers, white shirts, jeans, and leather vests (some with sleeves, some without)
  • Armed with tire irons, wrenches, claw hammers, and other blunt weapons
  • Low-ranking enforcers who control territory through intimidation
  • Extort locals and muscle out rival groups

Armored Bikers (Elite)

  • Heavily armored in boots, leather pants, shoulderpads, and gloves
  • Wear road goggles and motorcycle helmets
  • Tougher and more disciplined than common thugs
  • Serve as the backbone of offensive and defensive operations
  • Break enemy lines, protect retreats, engage the most dangerous opponents

Faction Conflicts

vs. The Community

The Death’s Head has been pressuring the Community, extorting its residents and controlling access to resources. The Community has resisted but is outmatched.

vs. The Stray Dogs

The Stray Dogs, sent by The Thumb from District 22, invaded Death’s Head territory following the L Corp. collapse. The Stray Dogs used the Pound as a recruiting pipeline, creating tension. The Death’s Head reluctantly maintained the arrangement under Stray Dogs’ orders. By Day 15, the Stray Dogs killed the local Death’s Head leadership — a shotgun woman and her second-in-command, found decapitated and hanging from streetlights. The remaining Death’s Head members fled.

vs. Agent Es

An unknown figure who goes around slaughtering the Death’s Head and wearing their gear to sow confusion and fear. His goal is the destabilization and overturning of the Death’s Head rule. He killed Road Captain Velder.

vs. Kurokumo Clan

The Kurokumo Clan, another subsidiary of The Thumb, killed Road Captain Jodi for failure to keep her territory in line. This was done to set an example following the death of Stray Dogs Packleader Bosco — a show of The Thumb’s displeasure with the Death’s Head’s inability to maintain order.

Status (Post-Day 15)

Effectively neutralized. The local leadership is dead or fled. The Pound is abandoned. Members are scattering before the Stray Dogs. The territory is in collapse.
