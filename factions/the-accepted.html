The Accepted

A body of people who gather relics. They wear ordinary clothes cut apart and sewn back together, and they speak of a woman they call She.

Appearance

Individual members dress in the same manner. Bright coloured fabric is sewn in patches over ordinary civilian clothing: tie-dye shirts, green towels draped across the shoulders, stitched and quilted pieces that read as makeshift robes. Each carries a book at the belt. One man wore papers tagged to his clothes and held a rusted knife.

The robes change under the relic. A man carrying the skull mace took a crown of thorns on his head and his robes turned red and white as it lit.

Doctrine

The Accepted believe relics are objects to be communed with, and that communion leads somewhere they call ascension. Their first words to strangers are an offer to help them commune deeper, and their request is only to be permitted to watch.

A member described it this way. Those who resonate with a relic should commune and reach the heights they were meant to. No one is made to stay: after communing, a person goes out and does as they please and paints the City in their own colours, as She meant it. They claim not to know what other relics the body holds, and say they will gather them regardless.

Two phrases carry the creed. Everyone is Accepted. We will go where the relics go.

She is the figure the relics point back to. A buyer among them described Her as the one who will set them free of powerlessness, said she was not foolish enough to stare at the sun too long, and wished aloud to be enveloped in Her embrace. When her arm was taken in a fight she said the wound was not Her blessing.

Conduct

They are quick to fight, and they fight with what is at hand: rusted knives, scrap swords, rusted machetes. They will put a body between a blade and the person they are protecting, and one asked that an attacker be taken alive.

The relic does more than they can. A member who attunes to one gains its weapon and its marks, and a member will force an attunement in the middle of a fight and take the relic off the person it belonged to.

Green fog is part of their method. A choir standing in a plaza in the district sang under a green tint in the air, and a man walking into it lost an hour to a vision that pulled his own memories up in front of him. In a fight, a member carried a device like a metal thermos with a funnel and bellows and sprayed with it, and the man it was used on weakened as it came.

Known members

  • Accepted Disciples, the rank and file, met at the cistern storage unit, in the plaza, in the shopping district, and at the auction house.
  • A buyer in a brown patchwork cloak who keeps several journals at her belt, identified by her own account as one of them.

Recorded encounters
