Day 06 Summary

District 12, Post-Collapse — In-World Day 6 Agents: Pug (Welfare Department), Burr (Disciplinary Department) The longest day of the campaign. Agents cross into Brotherhood territory, raid the Founder’s apartment, fight a cyborg, Burr bargains with his life, enters the Pound prison, massacres inmates, escapes with a cloaked ally, reunites with Pug and hired Fixers, and fights a Stray Dog packleader.

Key Events

  • 5 AM wake-up at Koller’s workshop. Agents depart The Time Machine.
  • Morning fight with Death’s Head patrol near the clinic. Burr recognizes the father of a man he killed. All three subdued alive.
  • Checkpoint massacre discovered — the axe-man’s work. Burr finds Death’s Head gear in a dumpster. Puts it on.
  • Raid the cafe kitchen. Food obtained. Cafe investigated for the scent on the helmet.
  • Return to the Community. Death’s Head gear confiscated briefly, then retrieved. Bread knife and other knives retrieved from locker.
  • Meeting with Ben. Pug gives bread. Burr gives half-eaten tin of oats. Ben provides locations for the workshop key.
  • Retrieve gear from armory. Borrow baseball bat (Burr) and PVC pipe spear with knife (Pug) for impeding authorities.
  • Scavenger market encounter. Warehouse district. Death’s Head cookout sighted.
  • Augmented checkpoint fight. Burr kills two. Harness augments fail.
  • Enter Brotherhood territory. Find the Founder’s apartment. Extensive looting — bayonet, keys, tools, cash, sealed containers, pills.
  • Cyborg fight in an alley. Burr acquires a damaged chainsaw sword. Pug burns his palm.
  • Burr captured at a reinforced Death’s Head checkpoint after fighting Brick (sergeant-at-arms).
  • Pug escapes through alleys, enters a pawn shop. Meets Kurisu (Grade 6 Fixer, Abyss Hunter Office).
  • Pawn shop fight. Kurisu defeats Death’s Head thugs. Pug sustains upper arm bruise.
  • Pug meets the Abyss Hunter Office: Pez, Dust, and Kent. Contract signed for 75,000.
  • Burr interrogated by a tired Death’s Head interrogator. Bargains his life with Stray Dog intel. Sent to the Pound (abandoned school prison).
  • Burr massacres the Pound: kills two inmates at the entrance, four in a classroom, meets the eyepatch man (blonde, one eye, searching for his compound bow), meets the ski-mask man (black tank top, one-hole mask), kills two thugs alongside him, fights through the broadcasting room against the Pound Boss. Acquires cloak, nailgun, and shield from a storage closet.
  • Pug and the Abyss Hunters infiltrate the Pound. Explosion at the back wall. Fight through the school entrance hall — Pug kills a guard and two inmates. Dust reveals claw augmentations.
  • Reunion — Pug and Burr find each other inside the school. Burr hands the nailgun to Pug. Ski-mask man is patched up.
  • The group exits the school to find three dozen Death’s Head blocking the way. The back alleys are sealed with sheet metal.
  • Pez explains the Head’s taboo — residential buildings cannot be breached at night. Dust squeezes through a gap.
  • The Death’s Head wave hits. Pug kills three, Burr kills two. Pez throws an explosive charge — shockwave scatters the enemy.
  • The Stray Dogs arrive. A swordsman in a suit and the tattooed Stray Dog Packleader. The swordsman negotiates a duel.
  • Final battle. Kent gives Burr and Pug protective bracelets. The fight is brutal — Pug’s forearm is fractured, his shield is destroyed. Kent is killed. Kurisu unleashes her case’s contents: an anti-material rifle, shotgun, and dual pistols. The Stray Dog’s arm is blown off. He falls.
  • The group is freed. The ski-mask man thanks Burr and departs.
  • Return to Diluc’s Tavern. Payment of 75,000 made. Change returned. 10,000 paid for a room.
  • Night. A muffled explosion from Pez’s room. Burr investigates. Dust — revealed as a small, pale girl — answers the door, a smoking Pez behind her. She accepts a headpat and closes the door.
