Summary

Retracing their steps with heightened senses and memory, they move hastily. Spotting potential threats before they take note, understanding when to back off and reroute, when to hide and let pass. It takes a fraction of the time — a better route devised through growing familiarity. Just barely under an hour to return to The Time Machine.

The elevator rides smoothly, no catching or hitching this time.

Stepping out into the workshop, they are greeted by the sight of Koller on the floor, face down. He lets out a large sigh, then quickly jumps to attention, flipping over and righting himself into a sitting position. Oh it is you two pritel! You are back? Already? He gazes at the package, then looks up at their state. Huh. You two get hit by truck on the way? This is way ahead of expectations, good job pritel! He pauses, then pales. What kind of tattoos?

Koller listens as they describe the Stray Dogs encounter. You two are lucky to be alive. Too many clients turned to ground beef by those dogs.

He takes the package graciously and heads to his computer. He has been doing research. Searching for easy jobs for new Fixers. Lost cat — pay seems OK. Bodyguard duty — many want it, few takers. A man wants volunteers to check abandoned structures. Something to do with the collapse. Koller advises against it — the man preys on greed. Small jobs, lay low, build up, stay alive.

Koller does not have much to give them. He can install augments for a fee. For weapons and gear — buy from shops or take from enemies. The treasure hunt starts the day after tomorrow. Always last minute.

Pug brings up the E.G.O. handgun. Koller recognizes it. He explains that he and many others were unable to crack one open. On close inspection it really should not work — and yet it does. No trace of manufacturing. One thing they figured out: the safety automatically engages when the last round is chambered, preventing the user from firing the final bullet without manually toggling the safety off.

Koller tries to reach a former client who stole ink from the Stray Dogs. He sends a message. Two hours pass. A client comes in and delivers a package to Koller. No message back from the former client.

They wait a couple more hours. Koller plays games, works on prosthetics, does research. The clock hits 8 PM. No message from the former client.

Pug shows Koller the key details about his prosthetic situation. Koller asks them not to make staying over a habit. Night falls in the Backstreets.
