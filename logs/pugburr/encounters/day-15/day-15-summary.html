Day 15 Summary

Timeline: December 6, 2025 to June 5, 2026

Time has lost all meaning. The characters have lost track of what day it is — hence the 1X- prefix and the title “Or is it.” This “day” spans six months and nearly 20 encounters.

Day 10 covers three distinct phases across six months: hospital discharge and the fall of Death’s Head (Dec 6), the Stray Dog investigation through messengers and oracles (Jan 3-17), and the explosive reunion with Lavender and the assault on Docker’s compound (Jun 5). Major revelations include Lavender’s identity as the raincoat figure, the nature of Enkephalin, and the introduction of The Accepted faction.

Encounter Summary

Phase 1: The Fall of Death’s Head (Dec 6, 2025)

Time Skip: ~4 months (Aug 11 to Dec 6, 2025). Burr has fully recovered from all physical injuries. Both characters have been living in The City. Pug’s symptoms — body weakness, mind fog, hand tremors — have persisted and worsened.

1X-1A: An Unknown Dawn (Dec 6) Leaving the hospital on Dec 6, both characters pay off their bills — the mysterious tax forms and the hospital bills — leaving them with nothing. Both independently notice strange new symptoms: clammy skin, hand tremors, cold to the touch (no fever). They head toward Death’s Head territory. At a barrel fire, two bundled individuals reluctantly give Burr torn cloth scraps to wrap his feet.

1X-1B: Housekeeping (Dec 6) Walking through Death’s Head territory, the warehouses they once knew are now looted and destitute. Three scavengers are searching through boxes. Burr announces himself: “Hello!~ Housekeeping!” The scavengers are terrified — more of the Stray Dogs than of Burr. One tosses him a pair of shoes. They point the way forward.

1X-1C: The Pound Falls Silent (Dec 6) They approach a Death’s Head checkpoint — the guards back up and split off, giving wide berth. No toll collected.

At the Pound, they find two bodies hanging by their feet from streetlights — decapitated. Burr identifies them as the local Death’s Head leader (shotgun woman) and her second-in-command. The headquarters is abandoned. Desks and paperwork left behind. The prison cells where Burr was once kept are empty.

Key Intel: A young woman in a medical mask and kitchen apron explains everyone is leaving before the Stray Dogs return. She gives them directions to Maisner — an information broker who trades info for food and drink.

Death’s Head status: Leadership dead. Territory in decline. Members fleeing.

Phase 2: The Stray Dog Investigation (Jan 3-17, 2026)

Time Skip: ~4 weeks (Dec 6, 2025 to Jan 3, 2026)

1X-2A: The Pit (Jan 3) Following directions to Maisner, they navigate alleyways to find Lester’s shack (a cook who shooes them away) and The Pit — a massive crater where a building used to be, now a communal dumping ground. Maisner sits on a makeshift porch overlooking the Pit. His test: bring a bowl of goulash from Lester.

1X-2B: Scavenging in the Pit (Jan 3) They scale into The Pit to scavenge ingredients for Lester. Burr finds food scraps. Pug finds a tied restaurant waste bag (fermented, gases building). Lester is intoxicated by the fumes when they bring it — “That’s the good shit.” He agrees to make goulash, but needs one final touch: meat. He sends them to Vagna, an old magic woman.

1X-2C: Vagna and the Cat (Jan 3) Vagna is grieving — her cat Mittens just died. Burr offers to bury the cat. A dead cat thuds behind them. Pug has an idea: cat goulash. Lester accepts the fresh meat. They make a fake grave for Mittens. Lester delivers four bowls of cat goulash and two pink liquor shots (temporary confidence boost).

1X-2D: The Summoning (Jan 3) They return to Vagna with a bowl of goulash. She asks what they need. When they ask her to “summon” something, she produces a live rat. They leave the rat at Lester’s door and head to Maisner.

1X-2E: Maisner’s Intel (Jan 3) Maisner smells the goulash before they even reach him. He demands the peach alcohol (which they don’t have) — Burr blows his breath in Maisner’s face instead. Maisner scribbles down directions.

Stray Dog Intel:

  • Bosco (the one who croaked in their territory) was a Pack Leader
  • Packs have grunts; Pack Leaders don’t mess with each other’s territory
  • Pack Leaders answer to someone higher, but no one knows who
  • Different Stray Dogs have different tattoos, different brands, some made in-house
  • Tattoo weakness: Only works when activated — short bursts. Catch them off guard, “you put that mutt down.”
  • Accessories can mimic tattoo powers — “cloak and dagger shit”
  • Maisner knows someone with a hard-on for the Stray Dogs — if they fuck up the parlor and bring back something good, he’ll put them together
  • Directions lead to a Stray Dog tattoo parlor

Time Skip: ~2 weeks (Jan 3 to Jan 17, 2026)

1X-3A: The Blindfolded Man (Jan 17) Following Maisner’s directions through the slums, they encounter a man in a tattered business suit standing in an alley. Burr feels something uncanny about his footsteps — hears his own non-skipping footsteps continue after he starts skipping. The blindfolded man approaches, mouth moving inaudibly. About twenty paces away, he begins a countdown. They flee. A door they passed is now open. They reach a dead end — the “TATTOO PARLOR.”

1X-3B: The Tattoo Parlor (Jan 3-17) The tattoo parlor is on the seventh floor of a building (Summit Accounting, Blue Rock Holdings, a hair salon below). The artist recognizes Burr’s prepped state. Pricing:

  • Full body: 3,000,000 Ahn
  • Per limb pair (two-pattern): 750,000 Ahn
  • Single limb single-pattern: 250,000 Ahn (200,000 for regulars)

No credit without insurance, Syndicate backing, or Fixer license. Over ~2 weeks, the artist offers one single-pattern whole-limb enhancement on credit (pay double next time). Burr considers it but ultimately declines. They leave.

1X-3C: The Prescripts (Jan 17) Exiting the building, they encounter two figures: the blindfolded man and a teenage girl in a hoodie. They deliver messages:

  • The girl: Tears a newspaper clipping in half — gives the smaller piece showing a BBQ restaurant award. “I was told to.”
  • The man (oracle message): “Return to the place of loss and sorrow, and you will reclaim that which you have lost, before the ‘morrow.” (The Pound/square where Kent fell.)
  • The man’s prescript: “Match the loudest footsteps upon hearing the shattering of glass.” “Exit the alley and turn right, whereupon walk twelve hundred paces, or until the door closes.”

Both received their prescripts mysteriously — the man that morning while getting groceries, the girl in her mailbox days prior.

1X-3D: Karla the Great Witch (Jan 17) Retracing steps, they spot two thuggish individuals surrounding a smaller woman. Burr intervenes, killing both scavengers (solar plexus strike, elbow to jaw, finished off). The woman introduces herself: Karla the Great Witch (800 years old, healer). She claims she doesn’t fight — she heals after battle and can “yell really really loud.” She joins the party.

Time Skip: ~5 months (Jan 17 to Jun 5, 2026). Karla now travels with the party. Pug acquires a machete during this period.

Phase 3: Lavender, Enkephalin, and the Relics (Jun 5, 2026)

1X-4A: Lavender’s Offer (Jun 5) Karla is hungry. They go to the BBQ restaurant from the newspaper clipping — the same unmarked restaurant from 7-5A. Inside:

  • A blindfolded young man in a raincoat sits at the counter
  • A young woman with lavender hair sits at a table with three steaming meals

The blindfolded man takes off the raincoat and leaves. The woman claims the coat: Lavender reveals herself — she was the raincoat figure from the facility (7-5A, 7-4K). She sent the blindfolded messengers.

Lavender’s Offer: She knows about “lost” items from the facility: a skull mace and a glass dagger, plus Enkephalin (green bubbling liquid in capsules). “You drank it? It’s a miracle you made it out.” The items are held by Docker, a local pawn dealer planning to sell tomorrow. Two options: night raid on their hideout, or intercept transport (risk of buyers).

Karla introduced properly: She confirms she’s a healer, 800 years old. She accepts “Lavender” as a nickname. The four head out together.

1X-4B: Clearing the Way (Jun 5) Lavender navigates through side streets and alleys. She stops before a corner — Burr charges into a chainlink fence, rips it off the poles. Three terrified hoodlums behind it offer intel: Docker’s operation is heavily protected, arrived a couple days ago with boxes and construction supplies.

1X-4C: The Pawnshop Garage (Jun 5) They reach Docker’s compound — a barricaded apartment building. Splitting up: Pug to the back, Burr to the front.

Burr slams the maul against the reinforced front door — gonging sounds. Pug finds an open garage converted into a pawnshop with three men: a pawnbroker, an armed guard, and a customer. During the confusion:

  • Pug snaps the customer’s neck silently
  • Pug stabs the pawnbroker through the back of the neck — learns only Docker has the key (penthouse, top floor)

1X-4D: The Second Floor (Jun 5) Burr breaches the front door. A man runs upstairs — Burr catches him, drags him down the stairs, curbstomps him. On the second floor, three armed men await (metal pipe, wooden bat, rusted machete).

Burr’s Rampage:

  • First man: skull crushed with maul
  • Second man: ribs crushed, finished off while whimpering
  • Third man: uppercut disconnects neck from spine

Docker’s voice on speakers: “Stop! Stop! What do you want?! I’ll give you them! Just stop killing!”

1X-4E: The Penthouse (Jun 5) Pug ascends to the top floor. Burr checks outside — finds a car door, rips it off with the maul for use as a weapon/shield. Pug reaches the penthouse: Docker on a reclining couch with four guards:

  1. Baton guard (enhanced, red eye)
  2. Puffy jacket guard (metal fists)
  3. Crowbar guard
  4. Tanto guard (Stray Dog tattoos)

1X-4F: The Penthouse Fight (Jun 5) Pug fights all four guards solo while Burr is outside:

  • Baton guard: Charged enhanced; Pug deflects with knife, slashes throat
  • Crowbar guard: Pug stabs wrist, jams knife up through jaw
  • Tanto guard (Stray Dog): Pug fires ballistic knife into his chest; used as human shield — killed by puffy guard’s metal fist (collateral)
  • Puffy jacket guard: Pug fires ballistic knife into his forehead — thunks deep into head

Losses: Machete (thrown, not retrieved), Slim Dagger / Ballistic Knife (fired twice, lost). Back slashed but armor blocked damage.

Docker surrenders: “Here! This is it! Storage unit outside!” He gives Pug a key necklace.

1X-4G: The Storage Unit and The Accepted (Jun 5) Docker leads them to a storage unit. Inside: journals, crates, and the relics:

  • Skull Mace — Long cross with red thorns binding and adorning a skull. When Burr grips it: crown of thorns sensation inside his skull. Does not draw blood despite thorns penetrating.
  • Glass Dagger — Shattered glass with a shifting pattern
  • 3 Yellow Enkephalin Canisters — Green bubbling liquid

The Accepted Introduced: Half a dozen individuals arrive — civilians wearing bright colored fabric patches, carrying books at their sides. Their leader: “We heard your calls of despair and anguish.”

They ask if Burr has communed with the relics. When he confirms the crown of thorns sensation, their eyes widen: “You must come with us. We can help you commune, to accept it deeper.” They seek to collect relics and help those who resonate with them most. “Everyone is Accepted.”

Lavender’s Reaction: Lavender tenses upon seeing the Accepted, reaching for her weapon. She warns: “That’s very much not a good idea.” She cannot speak freely in their presence. She is very wary of them.

Karla’s Reaction: Karla seems to enjoy the Accepted: “They remind me of my magic.” She suggests resting with them.

Departure: Docker is released and flees. Burr takes Karla and runs off. Pug tells Lavender they’ll meet her at the restaurant. The three head to the restaurant. The Accepted follow — now bound to the relics.
