Summary

The two follow Maisner’s directions, setting off immediately through the slum quarters. The ruined buildings gradually give way to undisturbed streets, the classic winding of backstreets they have grown accustomed to. A small side street marked by the directions appears ahead, clear and unremarkable.

As they proceed, Pug spots a man standing in an alleyway to their right. From behind, he wears a threadbare, tattered business suit. His head is slightly slumped, staring at the ground. The agents pass the alley warily. He does not react.

Burr continues walking. His foot strikes a glass bottle. It skitters forward, clatters across the pavement, tips over, and rolls into the street. They both pause, glancing back. Nothing emerges from the alley. They continue onward, nearing the end of the directions.

Then Burr feels it. Something uncanny about his footsteps. He freezes. Another footstep sounds in perfect sync with his own, and stops when he does. Looking behind him, the street has curved but still offers a long view back. No one is visible, but the sound had definitely come from behind, from some distance.

His senses heighten. He hears and feels nothing save Pug’s presence. He taunts whatever follows to reveal itself. No response.

He begins to skip down the road. Pug hears only Burr’s skipping. But Burr, faintly, hears the other footsteps resume their normal walking cadence, now even further away. He stops, pivots, and sprints toward Pug. Pug sidesteps, watching him oddly as Burr sprints past. The streets are so empty that his footsteps produce an echoing sound. He runs until he disappears from Pug’s view.

And then he sees it. The man from the alleyway, walking down the sidewalk toward him. This time, Burr can see his face. His eyes are wrapped in bandages, completely covered. He does not seem to notice or mind Burr’s increasingly loud footsteps closing the distance. He continues walking directly toward Burr, his mouth moving, but whatever he says is inaudible.

Pug, meanwhile, freezes as he hears a similar footstep nearby. It is coming from inside the building next door, one floor above. The footsteps descend a staircase. Pug leaves his position and heads toward Burr, glancing over his shoulder. No one emerges to follow.

Burr activates his hammer, holding his ground. The blindfolded man continues his steady approach, gait unwavering, muttering inaudible words. When he is about twenty paces away, he stops. The muttering continues, barely audible. Burr hears a countdown. Pug relays it.

They break off running. The man makes no move to follow. As they pass the building Pug heard the footsteps from, the door stands open. Yet no one appears on their way back.

Approaching the end of the street, they find it terminates in a dead end. Fortunately, one of the buildings bears a sign: “TATTOO PARLOR.” Burr barges inside. Pug waits outside.
