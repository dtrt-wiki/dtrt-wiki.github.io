Day 07 Summary

Timeline: July 23 to August 6, 2025

Day 07 covers a sprawling journey through the Fallen Wing underground facility beneath the Welfare Department building, followed by forays into Koller’s bunker, the community gate siege, and Burr’s hospitalization. The day begins with Pug descending alone into the depths and ends with both agents destitute in a rundown room and a clinic bed.

Key Locations: Fallen Wing underground facility (TV Room, Flesh-Hall, Cafeteria, Information Department, Armory, Spider Room, The Old Man’s chamber), BBQ restaurant, Koller’s bunker, Death’s Head checkpoint, Community gate, Time merchant, Organ harvester alleys, German doctor’s clinic

Encounter Summary

Phase 1: The Fallen Wing (7-1A through 7-2I)

Covered in original Day 07 Summary

Pug descends into the Fallen Wing alone, navigating the underground facility beneath the Welfare Department. A series of tense encounters through dark corridors, locked doors, and the abandoned offices of the lower floors. Burr eventually catches up. Together they navigate bureaucratic obstacles, discover the manager’s apology letter detailing the building’s descent into cannibalism, find the hidden elevator keys, and prepare for the deeper descent.

Phase 2: Descent into Darkness (7-3A through 7-3I)

7-3A-7-3C: The Sealed TV Room & Burr’s Memory Pug and Burr descend into the deeper facility. They discover a sealed TV room where Burr experiences a vivid memory — a fragmented vision of his past. The room is bathed in an eerie green glow from strange liquid permeating the facility.

7-3D-7-3F: The Flesh-Hall and Cafeteria The pair navigate a horrifying corridor lined with organic material — the Flesh-Hall. Beyond it lies an abandoned cafeteria, long since stripped of supplies. The green liquid continues to appear in various forms throughout these areas.

7-3G-7-3I: Burr Found, Vending Machine, Secret Elevator Pug finds Burr in a compromised state. They discover a strange vending machine that dispenses healing — a violet soda that miraculously restores wounds. Pug uses Martin’s Control Team ID card (retrieved earlier from the locker room) to access the machine. They find a hidden elevator leading deeper into the facility’s lower levels.

Phase 3: Information Department & The Old Man (7-4A through 7-4L)

7-4A-7-4C: Information Department, Forgotten Murderer, Samurai Armor The pair enters the Information Department — a vast archive of records and data. They encounter the Forgotten Murderer, a ghostly figure in a noose, whose suit proves to be a significant item. They also discover a sentient set of Samurai Armor — partially damaged but still animate.

7-4D-7-4F: The Old Man Boss Fight Deep within the department, they face The Old Man — a formidable boss. The fight is devastating:

  • Pug loses his left arm during the battle
  • Burr has his legs shattered Despite the grievous injuries, they manage to escape rather than finishing the Old Man off.

7-4G-7-4H: Frantic Retreat and Vending Machine Healing After barely escaping the Old Man, they frantically retreat through the corridors. They reach the same vending machine from earlier — the violet soda heals their worst wounds, allowing them to continue. Pug’s arm regenerates enough to function, Burr’s legs reform.

7-4I-7-4K: The Armory, Spider Room, Sir Tatum’s Appraisal They discover the facility’s Armory, containing powerful items:

  • Skull Mace — A long cross with a skull and red thorns (later revealed as a relic)
  • Dark Funeral Suit — Two-piece suit with a choking black miasma
  • Glass Dagger — A dagger with a shattered shifting pattern (later revealed as a relic)
  • Lever-action rifle and other items

They pass through a Spider Room — a chamber infested with a swarm of spiders, from which Burr retrieves bloodstained bills. Sir Tatum (a mysterious figure) appraises their found items.

7-4L: The Exit The pair finally escapes the underground facility, emerging back into The City. They are battered, exhausted, but alive — carrying several powerful relics and items.

Phase 4: The City Above (7-5A through 7-5B)

7-5A: The BBQ Restaurant Pug and Burr stop at an unmarked BBQ restaurant to eat and recover. Here they encounter a mysterious woman in a raincoat — later revealed to be Lavender. She observes them but does not engage directly.

7-5B: Rescuing Rune The pair come across a textile trader named Rune being attacked by a gang of Rat Thugs. They intervene:

  • 5 Rat Thugs killed total (Pug: 2, Burr: 3)
  • Rune is rescued
  • Payment received: 400,000 Ahn from the grateful trader

Phase 5: The Community and the Siege (7-6A through 7-6C)

7-6A: Koller’s Bunker They visit Koller’s underground bunker. Koller outfits them with new gear:

  • Car Battery Maul — A heavy electrical maul (Burr’s signature weapon)
  • Railroad Spike Dagger — Forged from a railroad spike, curved blade with brass knuckle grip
  • Light Armor Set — Long leather duster with metal and rubber reinforcement
  • Slim Dagger — Later revealed to be a ballistic knife

7-6B: Finding Ben They find Ben beaten and bloodied near the Death’s Head checkpoint. The checkpoint guards confront them, leading to a tense standoff.

7-6C: Community Gate Siege The community gate comes under siege. Burr is electrocuted during the fighting — severe electrical burns covering his body. Ben is captured by enemy forces. The siege ends with Burr critically injured.

Phase 6: Medical Emergency (7-7A through 7-7D)

7-7A: The Time Merchant Desperate for resources, Pug seeks out a Time Merchant — paying 50,000 Ahn for time multiplication. Burr’s badly injured calf receives basic treatment.

7-7B: The Organ Harvesters While navigating the alleys, they encounter organ harvesters — a grim reminder of The City’s brutality. They avoid engagement.

7-7C: The German Doctor’s Clinic Burr’s condition deteriorates. They find a clinic run by a German-accented doctor who admits Burr for emergency treatment:

  • Payment: 250,000 Ahn
  • Treatment: Heliapoiesis and fibrous mechanite therapy (dual treatment, never trialed together before)
  • Time Skip: 4 days of recovery
  • Burr receives a mechanical healing implant (gestator)

7-7D: The Midnight Visitor While Pug rests in his rundown room, a dark humanoid figure visits in the night. The visitor delivers tax documents — official paperwork showing Income: 100,000 SAU, Tax Due: 25,000 SAU. The figure departs without a word.
