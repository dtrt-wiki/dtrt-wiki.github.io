Summary

Inside the ruins, a staircase descends into complete darkness. The agents hold the railing, navigating by touch through the pitch-black corridor. Pug retrieves and switches on the flashlight.

Pug yanks the first door open. A string snaps — a metal can falls from above and clatters across the floor. A simple alarm trap. Nothing follows.

The first floor is a large looted office space: toppled cubicles, tipped water dispensers, emptied of anything valuable.

The second floor has a fire burning inside — warm air gusting out. Burr drops low and creeps toward the light. Two dead bodies: one on the floor, one slumped at a desk. Behind the body on the floor, under the desk, a small drawstring backpack sits. Burr retrieves it. Contents: a small tin of food, several staplers, a coffee mug, and a mostly used office tape dispenser. He leaves the water bottle behind as too risky.

A sound behind them near the fire. They withdraw and head to the third floor.
